How it works

Ella Robot sees every nail and paints it with 1 mm precision.

01

Rest

Your hand sits in a guided finger rest that holds each nail steady.

02

See

An overhead camera and vision models map the edge of every nail, then plan the brush path.

03

Paint

A precision arm brushes on base, color and top coat, cuticle to tip.

04

Swap

Sealed pods change colors with no mess and no cleanup.
